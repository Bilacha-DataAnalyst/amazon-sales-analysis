Amazon Sales Analysis & Power BI Dashboard 
​Executive Summary 
​This project analyzes Amazon sales performance to uncover revenue trends, top-selling product categories and key business insights. The raw sales data was cleaned, preprocessed and transformed to power an interactive Power BI dashboard (AMAZON.pbix). 
​Data Cleaning & Preprocessing 
​Removed duplicate transaction records to ensure accurate revenue calculations. ​
Handled missing values across product categories and pricing fields. 
​Standardized date and currency formats for consistent time-series tracking. 
​Engineered custom columns and DAX measures for key performance indicators (KPIs). 
​Key Insights & Observations 
​Top Revenue Drivers: Specific product categories consistently generate the majority of total sales volume. 
​Sales Trends: Revenue spikes noticeably during peak seasonal periods and promotional events. 
​Order Patterns: High-value orders correlate with specific customer segments and payment types.
Strategic Recommendations
Increase stock levels for top-performing product categories 4–6 weeks prior to identified peak sales periods to prevent stockouts. ​
Targeted Marketing: Focus promotional efforts on mid-tier products that show high order volume but lower overall margin. ​
Customer Retention: Create targeted bundling strategies for frequently co-purchased items to boost average order value (AOV). ​
Tools & Technologies 
​Power BI: Data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive visual reporting. 
​Data Cleaning: Excel for initial dataset auditing and cleaning.
